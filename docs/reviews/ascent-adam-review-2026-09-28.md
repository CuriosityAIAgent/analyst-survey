**The research behind 3, 4 and 19.**

- **EQ is not simply innate.** Personality is about 40% heritable, but EI and empathy training both work.
- **What predicts sales.** Selling knowledge predicts sales more than anything else, and drive is the strongest trait-like predictor.
- **So the cards are balanced.** Each side has one strength people mostly bring, one mixed and one mostly built. The answers therefore can't confirm "our best bankers are high-EQ" by design. We should report them as what graduates believe.
- **Why 19 asks about learning.** For AI, the differences between types show up in learning. AI that hands over answers lowered what learners could do alone. AI that gave hints did not. AI that passes on how the best people work sped novices up.
- **Sources.** They are in the code comments and in `ascent-evidence.md`.

**Length.** The new "today" screen (question 16) adds one question. The phone now has 19 questions (about 9 minutes) and the laptop 26 (about 13). Anyone who had started the old version starts again.

**For Adam to decide**

1. **Doing the work itself, with feedback.** This is not among the six kinds of experience in question 1, but the evidence ranks it the strongest source of learning. Should it replace one?
2. **Lists that are still over four and have no pictures.** These are 4, 7, 9, 11, 14 and 20 (six each), 5 (six, now with a line under each) and 13 (seven). They can be cut to four, or given small icons like the question 15 tiles. Icons would be a small design change.
3. **"Prospect outreach" in 15 to 17.** J.P. Morgan's Analyst job posting says Analysts research prospects rather than contact them. Should it read "Prospect research"?
4. **The 2031 scene and question 19 are written as "imagine".** Communications should approve the wording before it goes to Associates.
